This Privacy Policy explains how Conscious Canvas Movement (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit this website or register for our workshop.

Information we collect

  • Details you provide through our registration form: your name, email address, and WhatsApp/phone number.
  • Payment information you submit on our checkout provider’s page (processed by them, not stored by us).
  • Basic usage and device data collected automatically through cookies and analytics/advertising tools.

How we use your information

  • To register you for the workshop and send joining details, reminders, and the recording (if purchased).
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To send relevant updates and offers (you may opt out at any time).
  • To measure and improve our marketing, including advertising performance.

Third-party services

We share limited information with trusted service providers only to deliver our services, including our payment/checkout provider (TagMango), the video-conferencing platform (Zoom), our form/automation provider (Pabbly), and advertising and analytics platforms such as Meta (Facebook & Instagram). These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Cookies & tracking

This website uses cookies and the Meta Pixel to understand traffic and measure ad performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention & your rights

We retain your information only as long as necessary for the purposes above. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing shrutigoenka.artcoach@gmail.com.
